RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement of Lease is made and executed on this 1st day of October, 2024 at Pune, Maharashtra, by and between:

Mr. Rajesh Sharma (hereinafter referred to as the "LESSOR / LANDLORD", which expression shall include his legal heirs, executors, and assigns) of the ONE PART;

AND

Mr. Ayush Verma (hereinafter referred to as the "LESSEE / TENANT", which expression shall include his legal heirs, executors, and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the absolute owner of Flat No. 402, Sunshine Residency, Viman Nagar, Pune - 411014 (hereinafter referred to as the "Premises").

NOW THIS LEASE AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. Monthly Rent:
The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of INR 35,000 (Rupees Thirty Five Thousand only), payable strictly in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month via direct electronic bank transfer to the Landlord's account.

2. Security Deposit and Forfeiture:
The Tenant shall deposit a sum of INR 1,50,000 (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand only) as an interest-free refundable security deposit. In the event of any delay in monthly rent payment exceeding 7 (seven) days from the due date, the Landlord reserves the absolute and unilateral right to forfeit the entire security deposit of INR 1,50,000 without prior notice.

3. Immediate Unilateral Eviction:
The Landlord reserves the absolute right to terminate this agreement and demand the Tenant to vacate the premises within 24 (twenty-four) hours at any point during the tenancy without assigning any reason or providing prior notice.

4. Maintenance and Structural Repairs:
The Tenant shall be entirely responsible for all maintenance and repairs whatsoever, whether major, structural, or minor, including roof leakage, structural wall cracks, foundational dampness, electrical overhaul, plumbing issues, and building exterior repainting at the Tenant's sole expense.

5. Landlord Inspection and Entry:
The Landlord and their authorized representatives shall have the unfettered right to enter, inspect, or show the premises to third parties at any hour of the day or night without providing any advance notice to the Tenant.

6. Prohibition of Subletting:
The Tenant shall not sublet, assign, underlet, or transfer the possession of the premises or any part thereof to any third party without obtaining prior written consent from the Landlord.

7. Utility and Electricity Charges:
The Tenant shall promptly pay all utility charges including electricity, water consumption, and internet connectivity directly to the concerned authorities as per the actual meter readings.

8. Dispute Resolution and Jurisdiction:
Any dispute or controversy arising out of or relating to this agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent civil courts in Pune, Maharashtra.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have set their hands to this Agreement on the day, month, and year first written above.

LESSOR: Rajesh Sharma
LESSEE: Ayush Verma
